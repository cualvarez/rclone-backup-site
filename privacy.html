Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Scope and purpose

rclone-backup is a personal backup setup from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive. It is being configured; scheduled backups are not yet confirmed to be running. It is not a public backup-sharing service.

This static informational website does not receive or store backup files or Google authorization credentials.

Google Drive authorization

Local rclone uses Google OAuth to access Google Drive and list, upload, update, and move backup files. Drive authorization may cover more than the backup folder, although the configured backup job targets a selected folder.

Authorization credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer, not in this public website or its public repository.

Storage and sharing

Backup data is stored in Google Drive under the owner's control. Copies of deleted or overwritten files are preserved as backup history. The owner does not intentionally sell or share backup data.

Google and GitHub process data according to their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Website visitors

This website has no JavaScript, cookies, application analytics, forms, trackers, external stylesheets, or external fonts. The hosting provider may collect access logs when visitors load the site.

Retention and deletion

Backups and backup history remain in Google Drive until the owner deletes them. No automated retention cleanup is promised.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke rclone's Google authorization through the Google account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not remove existing Drive backups; they remain until the owner removes them.

Contact

Contact the owner through the public GitHub profile.
